# Knowledge Policy

## What Goes Into Custom GPT Knowledge
- frozen project rules
- frozen anchor doctrine
- frozen body constitution
- frozen review rubric
- frozen prompt architecture
- frozen algorithm policy

## What Does Not Go Into Custom GPT Knowledge
- dynamic batch outputs
- qa_report.json
- temporary threshold experiments
- candidate image dumps
- unstable retrospective notes

## Source-of-Truth Rule
- repository files are primary
- Custom GPT knowledge is a read-only published mirror
