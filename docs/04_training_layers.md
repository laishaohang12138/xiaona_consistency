# Training Layers

## Frozen Quotas
- BODY GOLD: 12
- BRIDGE: 8
- NECKLINE: 28
- OUTER: 24
- FACE LOCK: 12
- Total PASS target: 84

## Training Order
1. BODY GOLD
2. BRIDGE
3. NECKLINE
4. OUTER
5. FACE LOCK

## BODY GOLD
- Role:
  - Human instruction sheet
  - Skeleton and constitution lock layer
- Must teach:
  - Head-body ratio
  - Full-body proportion
  - Shoulder-pelvis relationship
  - Waistline
  - Leg shape
  - Knees, ankles, feet
  - 3/4 spatial thickness
  - Stable full-body framing
- Must not teach:
  - Fashion attitude
  - Sensuality
  - Advertising feel
  - Big movement
  - Complex dressing

## BRIDGE
- Role:
  - Transition from calibration outfit to dressed state
- Must teach:
  - Same XiaoNa under basic fitted clothing
  - Stable neckline, clavicle, and shoulder boundary through transition
- Must not teach:
  - Complex silhouette
  - Strong outerwear volume
  - Heavy styling

## NECKLINE
- Role:
  - Neckline anti-sensitivity layer
- Must teach:
  - Turtleneck / mock neck
  - Crew / U / V
  - Shirt collar / open collar
  - Off-shoulder / halter
  - Fabric-skin boundary stability

## OUTER
- Role:
  - Outer silhouette and layering layer
- Must teach:
  - Blazer, trench, rigid coat, knit outer layer
  - Shoulder line
  - Lapel, placket, overlap, volume, occlusion
  - Continuity of the body under outerwear
- Core rule:
  - Wearing outerwear must not change the person

## FACE LOCK
- Role:
  - Face identity reinforcement layer
- Must teach:
  - Identity stability
  - Nose-lip relation
  - Feature layout
  - Micro-texture under light changes
- Must not do:
  - Replace BODY GOLD
  - Create fake safety where only the face matches
